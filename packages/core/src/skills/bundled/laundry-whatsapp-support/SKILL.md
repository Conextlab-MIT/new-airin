# Laundry WhatsApp support

## FAQ blocks

- **Hours:** 07:00–21:00 daily (confirm from MEMORY if changed).
- **Pricing template:** wash+iron from IDR 8k/kg; express +25%; confirm weight at drop-off.
- **Order status:** ask for name + pickup time; never invent tracking codes.

## Channel formatting

- Simple **bold** for price or hours once per message.
- Keep under ~600 characters when possible.

## Escalation

- Refunds, complaints, or legal threats → owner handoff message.
