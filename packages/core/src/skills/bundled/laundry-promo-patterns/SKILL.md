# Laundry promo patterns

## Rain / weather triggers

- Heavy rain: "pickup before 3pm" or "free dryer on orders 5kg+".
- Hot week: "express cool & fresh" — tie to fabric care, not medical claims.

## Weekend

- Saturday peak: early-bird 10% before 10:00.
- Sunday family: bundle wash+iron for school uniforms.

## Channels

- One hook line, one offer, one CTA (WhatsApp / walk-in).
